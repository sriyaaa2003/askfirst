# v2: a better forecast and a learned review router

Same data, costs and conformal procedure as above; only the forecaster and the router change.

**Forecast.** `features2.py` adds 19 features that use only earlier weeks (a test checks this): longer and exponentially weighted history,
weeks since the last sale, price context, and demand of the same item in the other stores of the state and of the whole store-department.
Three Poisson gradient-boosted models (different sizes and seeds) are averaged.

| forecast | test WAPE | cost of acting alone, vs v1 |
|---|---|---|
| v1 (20 features, one model) | 0.3628 | 0 |
| + new features | 0.3608 | -0.9% |
| + larger model (3M rows) | 0.3578 | -1.5% |
| + three-model average | **0.3546** | **-2.3%** |

The gain is small. The 4-week-average baseline is 0.385, so the model is now 7.9% better than it, against 5.8% before.

**Learned router.** A gradient-boosted regressor is trained on the calibration weeks (outcomes known) to predict the realised benefit of a review from the
inputs of the Monte Carlo score plus the item's recent forecast errors and the store-department's last-week error. It is evaluated on the untouched test weeks.
Share of the all-review saving captured:

| policy (v2 forecast) | 2% budget | 5% | 10% | 20% | 30% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monte Carlo value of review (v1 router) | 17.5% | 28.1% | 39.6% | 55.2% | 66.5% |
| **learned router** | **18.4%** | **29.3%** | **41.1%** | **56.9%** | **68.2%** |
| oracle | 24.9% | 38.3% | 52.1% | 69.0% | 79.5% |

The learned router adds about 1.5 points at a 10% budget and closes about 12% of the gap to the oracle. It is a modest gain, not a new regime.
Two things that did not help: refitting the router every 8 weeks on the outcomes seen so far (41.04% at 10%, the same as fitting once), and
scoring the 80% interval width, which stays at 5% (worse than random). The 12-point gap to the oracle remains: most of what makes a review valuable
(the realised error) is not predictable from what is known before the week.

Reproduce: `python experiments/forecast_models.py`, `python experiments/learned_router.py ens_feat results/v2/router_ens.json`, `python experiments/forecast_cost.py`.
